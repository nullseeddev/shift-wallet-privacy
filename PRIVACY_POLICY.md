Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy describes how NullSeed ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application Mesai Kumbarası / Shift Wallet (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect
A. Personal Data (Local Storage Only) Our App is built with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal work data on any external server. All data created within the App (shift records, wage settings, templates, etc.) is stored exclusively and securely on your device using an offline database. Your database is strictly encrypted locally using AES-256 encryption. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it.

B. Backup & Restore The App offers a Backup & Restore feature. When you create a backup, the App securely encrypts your data using PBKDF2 AES-256 encryption with a password of your choice, and saves it as a physical file on your device's local storage.

Important: We have no access to your data or your backup passwords. If you lose your password, your exported backup file cannot be recovered.

C. Non-Personal Data (Third-Party Services) We use Google AdMob to display advertisements, which may collect Device identifiers, Usage and diagnostic data, IP addresses, and Cookies. We do not have direct access to this data. It is collected and processed by Google in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy We also use Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous diagnostic data, crash reports, and usage statistics to improve application stability.
2. Data Deletion and Retention
Since we do not store your data on our servers, there is no account deletion process required on our end. You have complete control over your data:

Instant Deletion: You can use the "Hard Reset" (Delete All Data) feature inside the App's settings to securely and permanently wipe all your data from the device.
Uninstalling: Simply uninstalling the App from your device will also securely delete all associated local databases and encrypted keys.
3. App Permissions
Notifications: To display the ongoing shift timer.
Internet Access: Required solely to load advertisements via Google AdMob and send anonymous crash reports. Your personal shift data is never sent over the internet.
Vibration: Used for subtle tactile feedback.
Storage / Files: Required temporarily to save or load your encrypted backup files to/from your device's file system.
The App does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, or contacts.

4. GDPR & User Consent (EEA / UK Users)
For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You can change your ad personalization preferences at any time through your device's ad settings.

5. Data Security
Your personal data is encrypted on your device using industry-standard AES-256 encryption. We implement no server-side data handling because we collect none.

6. Children's Privacy
This App is designed for working adults to track their shifts and earnings. It is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically.
