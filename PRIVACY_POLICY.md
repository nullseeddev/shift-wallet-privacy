## English

### 1. Information We Collect
**A. Your data stays on your device.** Your shifts, wages, jobs, notes and settings are stored only on your device in an offline database encrypted with AES-256. The encryption key is kept in your device's secure storage. This data is never sent to us or to any server unless you explicitly export it.

**B. Backups.** You can export a backup file. If you set a password, the file is encrypted (PBKDF2 and AES-256); without a password it is not encrypted, and the app warns you. We cannot access your backups or passwords; if you lose the password, the backup cannot be opened. **Because your data is stored only on your device, backing it up is your responsibility.** The app shows a reminder about every 30 days. If you lose or reset your phone or uninstall the app, data without a backup cannot be recovered.

**C. Third-party services (technical and advertising data).**
- **Google AdMob** shows ads (banner and full-screen). Ads shown in the app are **not personalized**; they are not targeted to your interests. Google may process your device's advertising ID, IP address and cookies or similar technologies, under Google's own policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy), to prevent fraud, limit how often an ad is shown and create aggregated reports.
- **Firebase Crashlytics** collects anonymous crash and diagnostic data (device model, OS version, error logs).
- **Firebase Analytics** collects anonymous usage events (for example: setup completed, shift saved, backup created, records page viewed). Your shift contents, wages and notes are not part of these events. Analytics data may be linked to Google Ads only to measure how well our own app promotion campaigns perform (for example, install attribution).

### 2. Data Deletion and Retention
There are no accounts and no server copy of your data. You can delete everything with "Delete All Data" in the app, or by uninstalling it (this removes the local database and the encryption key). Backup files you created are stored where you saved them; delete them yourself. Data held by Google services is handled under Google's policies.

### 3. App Permissions
- **Notifications:** to show the ongoing shift timer.
- **Internet:** only for ads and crash/usage reports.
- **Advertising ID:** for fraud prevention, ad frequency limits and aggregated ad reporting.
- Backup files are saved or picked with the system file picker; the app does not request broad storage access.

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or vibration permissions.

### 4. Ad Choices and Consent (EEA / UK / Switzerland)
Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent form as required by law. You can review your choices at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Ads in the app are not personalized in any region.

### 5. Data Security
Local data is encrypted with AES-256. We do not operate servers that store your shift data. Network traffic is limited to the third-party services above.

### 6. Children's Privacy
The app is intended for working adults and is not directed at children under 18.

### 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy, for example when features or partners change. The latest version and its date are always shown here.

### 8. Contact
nullseeddev@gmail.com — NullSeed

Os dados locais são encriptados com AES-256. Não operamos servidores que armazenem os seus dados de turnos. O tráfego de rede limita-se aos serviços de terceiros acima indicados.

A app destina-se a adultos que trabalham e não a crianças com menos de 18 anos.
